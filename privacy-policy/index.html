Effective date: November 3, 2026

StaySmartTemplates ("we", "us", or "our") operates a digital product business and uses technology services to manage its Pinterest marketing, content creation, and business operations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and processed through our website and related services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features being used, we may process:

  • Pinterest account information made available through authorized API permissions.
  • Pin and Board information associated with our own Pinterest account.
  • Pin performance metrics, including impressions, clicks, saves, and other available engagement statistics.
  • Content information, product descriptions, design instructions, and creative materials used to produce promotional content.
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting us.
  • Technical information generated when visitors access our website.

2. Pinterest API Usage

We use the Pinterest API to manage our own Pinterest marketing activities. Depending on the permissions granted, our application may create and publish Pins, manage relevant content, and retrieve authorized performance metrics.

We use performance metrics to understand content effectiveness, compare historical results, and improve future Pinterest publishing strategies.

Our Pinterest API integration is intended for our own business account and marketing activities.

3. Local Database and Data Storage

Relevant Pinterest performance information is stored in a database hosted on our own Mac computer. We use these records to maintain historical performance data, compare results over time, and support our internal analytics.

We take reasonable steps to protect locally stored information and restrict access to authorized business operations.

Information is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. We review stored information and delete it when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

4. AI-Assisted Analysis and Content Creation

We use AI services to analyze Pinterest performance and improve our marketing content.

These services include:

  • Google Gemini: Used for performance analysis, content ideas, and AI-assisted image generation.
  • Anthropic Claude: Used for performance evaluation, strategic recommendations, and content development.
  • 9Router: Used to route requests to selected AI model providers.

Relevant performance metrics may be sent to AI service providers for analysis. We aim to limit these requests to the information needed for the specific analysis and do not intentionally include unnecessary personal information.

5. Canva API and Design Processing

We use Canva services and API functionality to create, edit, and organize promotional designs and Pinterest content.

Depending on the design task, relevant design instructions, product information, text, and creative materials may be transmitted to Canva to perform the requested design operations.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Our services rely on third-party providers, including Pinterest, Google, Anthropic, Canva, and 9Router.

When information is transmitted to these providers, it is processed under the applicable provider's terms, privacy policies, and service arrangements.

We do not sell Pinterest performance data or personal information to third parties.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by our services. These measures may include access restrictions, account security controls, and protection of locally stored business data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Requests and Pinterest Authorization

You may contact us with questions or requests concerning personal information that we process, subject to applicable law and our ability to identify the relevant information.

Access granted to our application through Pinterest can be revoked through the relevant Pinterest account settings.

9. Third-Party Websites
